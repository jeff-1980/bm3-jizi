\begin{itemize}
\item Freezing every input-dependent term of a Mamba-3 scan costs no deep-noise accuracy.
\item A fully input-independent scan beats S4D by 21-26pp in both directions.
\item Removing the multiplicative gate costs 9-24pp of macro-F1 in deep noise.
\item A same-size additive branch in place of the gate rescues nothing, in both directions.
\item Added to S4D, a gate recovers little more than an additive branch of the same size.
\end{itemize}
