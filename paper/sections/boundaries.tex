\section{Boundaries and Limitations}
\label{sec:boundaries}

We state the scope plainly.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Testbed scope.} All claims are bounded to bearing vibration from one pair of operating conditions of one dataset (37.5\,Hz/11\,kN and 40\,Hz/10\,kN; speed and load both change), binary classification, GPU execution (no edge/real-time claims). The pair is run in both directions, but the reverse direction uses the same eight bearings with their roles swapped and has a single IR bearing in its evaluation set, so it is not an independent replication; the third condition of the dataset has no IR bearing. The five seeds do not resample bearings (Section~\ref{sec:protocol:testbed}). Independent bearings, multi-class settings and a second dataset are future work.
\item \textbf{Attribution level.} Credit assigned to ``the rest of the composition'' is by elimination within the enumerated component set; pairwise interaction terms are not decomposed, and the host-by-gate contrast of Section~\ref{sec:results:graft} is a four-arm estimate that cannot be assigned to a specific component.
\item \textbf{The transplant conclusion is budget-conditional.} All arms share a matched 50-epoch budget; the grafted gate trains normally yet recovers little, and convergence is not robustness (Section~\ref{sec:results:graft})---but we cannot exclude that a longer or graft-tuned budget would recover more. Our statement is therefore limited to \emph{this gate branch, in this host, under a matched training budget}.
\item \textbf{Matched-SNR scope.} Training and evaluation share the same injected SNR throughout (Section~\ref{sec:protocol:testbed}); ``robustness'' in this paper is matched-noise robustness under noise-aware training, not clean-train/noisy-test generalisation. The gate's benefit may operate by exploiting matched-noise training (consistent with Section~\ref{sec:results:perclass}) rather than by test-time denoising; attribution under mismatched-noise protocols is untested and may differ.
\item \textbf{Graft dual reading.} Interaction dependence vs.\ transplant friction (Section~\ref{sec:results:graft}), the latter now subsumed under the budget condition of item~3.
\item \textbf{Noise-regime coverage.} Under strong coloured noise at $-10$\,dB a TCN baseline slightly overtakes the Mamba-style block, and $-10$\,dB is near-chance for the time-invariant baseline and excluded from adjudication; the demonstrated advantage regime is matched, Gaussian-dominated noise.
\item \textbf{Conv-stem dual reading.} Its clean-data cost admits both representation-mistransfer and optimisation-difficulty readings, reported in place (Section~\ref{sec:results:localise}).
\item \textbf{Reporting rule.} Reported values are final-epoch macro-F1 under a fixed 50-epoch budget. The original grids' best-epoch rule, for which the thresholds of the early grids were pre-specified, is reported as a sensitivity analysis and is optimistic by $0.6$--$10.6\pp$ (Section~\ref{sec:results:sensitivity}). Every arm of the chain was re-run on the three adjudication levels; the $+10$/$+6$/clean/$-10$\,dB levels and the per-class grid were not re-run and are auxiliary (best epoch). No source-domain validation rule was available, and because the final epoch of the same evaluation condition has been inspected repeatedly, final-epoch values are not a fresh confirmatory test.
\item \textbf{Scope of the freezing intervention.} The chain's first step freezes the input-dependent parts of $\Delta$, $A$, $B$, $C$ only; the fully input-independent scan is a separate grid (Section~\ref{sec:results:fullfreeze}) on the three adjudication levels, which was specified after the earlier grids had been seen. No equivalence margin was pre-specified for the cost of freezing, so ``no measurable cost'' means the point estimates favour the frozen arms with intervals including zero, not a demonstrated equivalence. Freezing is implemented inside this Mamba-3 layer; a conclusion about input-dependence in general would need other blocks and tasks.
\item \textbf{Gate interventions.} The gate-to-additive substitution has been made in both hosts and both directions. In the Mamba-3 block the verdict is \emph{not rescued} on both transitions, with upper interval bounds of $0.486$ and $0.362$ against a threshold of $0.5$ (the first close to it). In S4D the width-matched arms are larger than the donor block, and on the reverse transition the gate carries a small increment over the additive branch that it does not carry on the original transition; this direction dependence is unresolved.
\item \textbf{Strength of the pre-specification evidence.} Thresholds were pre-specified internally; for all but the last three follow-up grids the supporting evidence is local file times and run-directory names, not an external time-stamped registry (Section~\ref{sec:protocol:prereg}). The process-level repeat check is a single observation.
\item \textbf{Construction checks.} For the frozen arm the state-dictionary check compares names and shapes of non-target tensors; value-level comparison was applied to the component-ablation and graft arms only.
\item \textbf{Bearing identity.} All XJTU-SY transitions and the replicating Paderborn setting share bearings between training and evaluation, and in each a bearing's identity determines its class. On Paderborn bearings disjoint from the training set, every examined architecture, S4D included, transferred close to the majority-class level and none of the contrasts replicated (Section~\ref{sec:results:pu}). The conclusions are therefore limited to cross-condition drift with shared bearing identity; robustness to unseen bearings is not supported by these data.
\item \textbf{Sample size.} Every cell mean uses five seeds. The smallest attainable one-sided Wilcoxon $p$ is $0.031$, paired intervals are wide, and several secondary statements (conv stem, $A$ parameterisation, gateless versus S4D) are not resolved at this size; conclusions rest on effect sizes that clear the pre-specified thresholds, not on significance tests, which were never adjudication criteria.
\end{enumerate}

A single-repeat check of process-level non-determinism ($\sim\!0.7\pp$) and the label-hash/adjudication trail are documented in the reproducibility appendices (\ref{app:fairness}--\ref{app:confusion}).
